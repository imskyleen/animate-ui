# Indicator (Tabs, Toggle Group, Pagination, Navigation Menu, Sidebar active item)

Imports and base-library wiring: [README.md](README.md).

Purpose: spatial, the selection moves between items.

- A shared element with `layoutId` (Animate UI's `Highlight` in children mode) on `spring.selection`: the new surface slides from the previous one. Hand it over: the previous copy goes in the same frame and the new one starts at full opacity, so one surface moves. Only a highlight appearing from nothing fades in, and one going away with no item taking it fades out. Two copies crossfading (the old one fading out inside `AnimatePresence` while the new one fades in) shows a doubled surface on a translucent or bordered highlight. A spring keeps fast clicking smooth: it retargets with its velocity.
- The keyboard moves it like a click.
- The labels stay above it: `relative z-10` on each label. Without it, a surface moving to a later item passes over the earlier items' labels (it's later in the DOM). Check a frame mid-move.
- Never follow an item by animating `top`, `left`, `width` or `height`.
- Base UI Tabs don't select on focus since 1.0 (`activateOnFocus` is `false`): arrow keys move focus, Enter or Space selects.
- `ReducedMotionConfig` around the Root turns the `layoutId` move into a jump.

### Tabs (internal/tabs)

The tabs:

- One indicator, never two crossfading: a single surface sliding on `spring.selection`, a Web Animation replacing its `translate` (from its own plus the offset: on the compositor, so the panels changing never hold it up; `composite: 'add'` would run on the main thread) and of its `width`/`height` only when the tabs' sizes differ (a scale would distort the copied border, radius and shadow). Picked up on the way (quick clicks, arrow keys), it goes on from where it is with the speed it had (internal/momentum), from the frame it was read at. It copies the active tab's classes and data attributes and hides the tab's own surface. All of this is the library tabs' (Radix UI, Base UI, React Aria): Animate UI's own Tabs keep v1's motion, by the user's choice.
- Tab text color: `transition.color` (200ms, `ease`), whatever the tabs' own transition.
- Panels one after the other, like `AnimatePresence` `mode="wait"`: the outgoing panel fades out into `blur.panel` on `transition.panelOut` (150ms, `ease.leave`), kept mounted, in the page and `inert`; the incoming one waits `hidden` until it's gone, then fades in from `blur.panel` on `transition.panelIn` (300ms, `ease.enter`) while its height glides from the outgoing panel's to its own on `transition.panelHeight` (450ms, `ease.resize`: Motion's layout curve), clipped on the way. While the switch settles (its glide, then one follow), its content changing size (loaded or added late, a nested animation settling) is followed: watch the gliding element's children (a ResizeObserver, its first report included) and its subtree (a MutationObserver: new children, text, inline content), and glide from where it is and at its speed (`velocity` read by seeking it back a few ms) to the new height on `spring.morph` (internal/momentum `curveFrom`), never ending on the stale height and jumping. A glide still waiting out its `delay` (a Questionnaire's next question) restarts on its own timing instead: its `currentTime` runs from 0 to `delay` while it waits, so test `currentTime < delay`, not `< 0` (true only in the frame it was made). Content resizing again within a few frames of a follow started at rest animates itself (an accordion opening, a CSS transition): let it go, unclamped, or the panel lags behind it and clips it. A margin escaping the gliding element's bottom (CSS stops it collapsing through once min/max-height change the height) glides along as its own `marginBottom`, measured with a probe that leaves the inline style as it was (no transition seeing it); else everything below jumps by it as the glide starts and ends. Animate UI's `TabsContents`: until its first height is on screen, it keeps its natural height and folds its inactive panes, so it's the shown panel's (on the server too, the first panel without `defaultValue`); a nested one mounted hidden applies its first height at once (grown from 0, it would change its panel's content on the way), an empty panel on screen is pinned at 0 so switching away glides. Changed again on the way: a panel passed over never shows, one leaving fades out from where it is, one picked again comes back from there, the height goes on from where it is.
- A panel holding a box (shadcn's Card) keeps the box on screen: find the panel's frame (the panel if it paints a border, a background or a shadow, else the first element down its chain of single children that does) and fade only its content (its child elements); the next panel's frame takes over at the same height in the same commit, then glides its own height (min/max-height, clipping only vertically, so its border, corners and ring are always drawn). Fading the whole panel blinks the Card's border, and gliding the panel's height cuts the Card's bottom edge. Decide when the panel starts leaving, from the panel coming in: frames that don't look the same (border, radius, background, shadow) or that hold text right inside fade whole. A `filter` makes the faded element the containing block of what's positioned inside it against an element outside it (Fumadocs' step badge): fade those without the blur.
- The content slides `shift.tabs` (12px) by the tabs' order, on the fades' own animations (one Web Animation per element: opacity, filter, `translate`), so it moves and fades as one gesture: toward the start leaving for a later tab, in from the end; the reverse going back; along Y for vertical tabs, mirrored in RTL. Turned back while it comes in, it goes back out from where it is. Under reduced motion, and for content `anchorsOutside` (a `translate` re-anchors it like a `filter`), no slide.
- Radix reports an uncontrolled value change from an effect, a commit late: in that commit a leaving panel is unmounted, then mounted again (losing its animations). Drive Radix's Root as controlled from your own state so the panels know the value in the same render.
- Keep a leaving panel mounted the library's way: Radix with `forceMount` (its Presence only waits for CSS animations); Base UI and React Aria wait for the element's running animations, so start the fade out as Web Animations in the commit it leaves in (a layout effect, before React Aria's own looks). Start each animation at the time it's made (`startTime`, `fill: 'backwards'`): left pending, it starts at the frame's time, which a long render leaves behind, and skips its first frames.
- Nothing moves on page load, nor before the page is painted (a value restored in an effect). Under reduced motion the indicator and the height jump; the panels still go one after the other, fading through their blur without sliding.

### Toggle Group (internal/controls/highlight)

Radix UI, Base UI and React Aria toggle groups. shadcn/ui paints the items themselves (`data-[state=on]:bg-muted`, `hover:bg-muted`): the group reads each item's own background and corners and paints them on its own surfaces, behind the items and under their borders, in the group's shadow root around a `<slot>` (children added to the group would break the items' `first:` / `last:` styles); a stylesheet clears the items' own backgrounds.

- Multiple selection: a selected item's surface grows in from `scale.fill` (70%, from its center) as it fades in, on `transition.fill` (300ms, `ease.fill`), and shrinks back out as it fades.
- Neighbours selected together join into one surface on `transition.fill`: each stretches to the middle of the gap (0.5px past it, so no seam shows) as its inner corners square off; deselecting one splits them. They join when they touch, or across a gap no wider than half their height when the facing sides have no border: bordered items apart keep their own surfaces (a band between two frames reads as a stray strip). Geometry, not transforms: the merge changes corners and lengths, which a scale would distort. Rows, columns and RTL from the items' boxes.
- Single selection: one surface slides on `spring.selection`; picked up on the way it keeps its speed (internal/momentum's `startOf`), started from the frame it was drawn on (a negative `delay`), else its first frame stalls. Grows in on the first selection, shrinks out when cleared.
- Hover: one highlight glides on `spring.highlight`, fades in from nothing and out on `transition.color` (back to full on `transition.fade` when taken over halfway), like a menubar's triggers. Lit only where the hover style paints something the selection doesn't; above the selected surfaces; it stays over an item clicked until the surface growing or sliding in under it has arrived (fading as the surface grows shows a dip), and lights at once over a surface deselected under it.
- A pressed item's surfaces follow its press scale (read from its box each frame while pressed).
- Reduced motion: fades only. A single selection crossfades between items; a fill fading in next to a selected one covers the gap (and the neighbour's rounded corners) as it fades; the highlight jumps.

### Pagination (primitives/pagination)

shadcn/ui's pagination has no library primitive (no import to swap): build its `<nav>` on `@animate-ui/react/primitives/pagination`'s `Root`; `PaginationLink` needs nothing (`data-slot="pagination-link"`, `aria-current="page"`). Same engine as the tabs' indicator (internal/sliding-indicator).

- One surface, the current link's (the Root's own, not a nested pagination's; drawn: a `display: none` duplicate is skipped): a `span` (`data-slot="pagination-indicator"`, `aria-hidden`, no pointer events, `box-sizing: border-box`) copies its classes and data attributes (the outline variant: border, background, radius, shadow, dark mode). Not `aria-current`: style the current page through the variant or `data-active`, never an `aria-[current=page]:` class. An unlayered stylesheet hides the link's own border, shadow and background once it's on screen (`data-animate-ui-pagination-ready`).
- Stacking: the Root is `isolation: isolate`, the surface its first child at `z-index: 1` (over the list's and items' backgrounds, positioned or not); once ready, the links and the ellipsis get `position: relative; z-index: 2` in `@layer base` (a link's own `absolute` wins), so they paint over it.
- Slides on `spring.selection`: a Web Animation replacing its `translate` (from its own plus the offset: composited; `composite: 'add'` would run on the main thread), `width`/`height` only when the links' sizes differ; picked up on the way, it keeps its speed toward the new page (internal/momentum).
- From where it was drawn, not from the old link's new place: page numbers shifting in the same commit don't make it jump; a current link staying put doesn't move it. Links measured without their own translate (px or %), scale or scale/translate-only transform, about their transform-origin (Button's `active:translate-y-px`, a `whileTap` scale); the Root's ancestors' scale exactly (its unrounded layout size, not `offsetWidth`).
- At rest it follows at once: the Root or a link resizing, text changing inside the Root (`characterData`), a list inside it scrolling (a capturing `scroll` listener). While the Root is drawn transformed (a dialog's entrance), the links are read again each frame (90 at most) until it's drawn at its own size.
- Hover and focus stay the link's: hovered (hover-capable devices), it paints its own hover background, and the surface's own background fades out under it on `transition.color` once it has arrived (kept while `data-animate-ui-sliding`), so the two don't add up (dark `bg-input/30` under `hover:bg-input/50`); a nested pagination's hovered link doesn't count. `:focus-visible`: its own ring.
- Nothing on mount, nor before the first paint: a pagination remounted by a navigation (a plain `<a href>`) shows the new page at once; client state or a search param read by the same page slides. In the frame the surface shows in, `data-animate-ui-pagination-settling` turns the current link's `transition` off (shadcn's `transition-all` would fade its border out over the surface, doubling it).
- Reduced motion: crossfades on `transition.fade` (a copy, `data-animate-ui-leaving`, fades out on the old link, the surface in on the new one). Changed again mid-crossfade, the new copy leaves from the surface's current opacity and the older copies go on.

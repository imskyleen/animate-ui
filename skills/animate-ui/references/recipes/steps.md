# Steps (Questionnaire)

Imports and base-library wiring: [README.md](README.md).

Purpose: spatial, the flow moves on one step (or back).

shadcn/ui's Questionnaire is built on `@shadcn/react/questionnaire`, whose drop-in is `@animate-ui/react/primitives/questionnaire` (same API; the CLI swaps it). The library keeps every step mounted (their answers are the form's) and hides the inactive ones (`hidden`, `inert`), its buttons and its errors too, in the commit that changes them. Don't re-render anything: the Root watches its parts' attributes (a MutationObserver on `data-active`, `data-checked`, `data-invalid`, `data-hidden`, its parts marked `data-animate-ui-questionnaire-part`), which reports before the frame is painted, and animates the elements with Web Animations.

- Steps one after the other, like the tab panels, sliding by their order: the leaving step is shown again (`hidden` removed) and pinned `position: absolute` (plus `margin: 0`, `grid-area: auto`, `box-sizing: border-box`) over the next one, on the next one's unrounded box (`getBoundingClientRect` against the parent's padding box, an ancestor's scale undone; `offsetLeft`/`offsetWidth` round, and a rounded width can rewrap text), still `inert`; its parent is made `position: relative` while something is pinned in it (if static). Save the element's own inline values of those properties and the parent's `position` before, and put them back after (React won't rewrite a style prop that didn't change); it slides `shift.step` (24px) toward the start as it fades out into `blur.panel` on `transition.panelOut` (150ms, `ease.leave`). The next one comes in from `shift.step` toward the end, out of `blur.panel`, on `transition.panelIn` (300ms, `ease.enter`), delayed by the exit with `fill: 'backwards'` (and by any step still leaving from a change just before): never hidden, so the library's focus lands on it and stays. Going back, the reverse; mirrored right to left; a jump over several steps goes straight there.
- Height: from the leaving step's to the next one's on `transition.panelHeight` (450ms, `ease.resize`), as `min-height`/`max-height` clipping vertically (internal/tabs `glideHeight`), delayed by the exit like the entrance and filling backwards: held at the leaving step's height until it has gone, so what's below doesn't slide over it, then moving on that curve. Content resizing on the way is followed from where the glide is, on `useTempoTransition(spring.morph)` (paced by the tempo like the rest). Reduced motion: the height changes at once, but only once the leaving step has faded out (the same delay, a 1ms glide).
- Changed again on the way, each goes on from where it is (read from its computed style): a step leaving again exits in `panelOut` × its opacity, one picked again while leaving comes back from where it is without waiting, the height goes on from where it is.
- Progress: the numbers of its default label roll, each a span whose own text stays the number (read, copied, announced: the progressbar is `aria-live`) but is painted transparent (`-webkit-text-fill-color`) while two pseudo-elements roll over it: `::before` (`data-animate-ui-from`, the old number) slides 0.6em away and `::after` (`data-animate-ui-to`, the new one) in from the other side, each through `blur.icon`, on `spring.slide` (300/35, never past) run through `toCssTiming` (a 750ms `linear()`); up when the number grows. Give them empty alternative text (`content: attr(…); content: attr(…) / ""`, the plain declaration first for browsers without it) so assistive tech reads only the span's text. Changed again mid-roll, the new `::before` starts from the old `::after`'s computed look. Reduced motion: a crossfade on `transition.fade`. A Progress inside each step (shadcn/ui's card and dialog examples) doesn't roll: the step coming in shows its own number, and the step leaving keeps its own (`data-animate-ui-hold`, painted by `::before`) until it's hidden. Custom children or `render` stay as they are.
- Choices: pressed, the choice (its `label`, rendered as `motion.label` through the library's `render`) scales to `scale.choice` (0.985, lighter than `scale.press`: a whole row) on `spring.press` (`usePressMotion({ scale })`). Their indicator animates like the radios and checkboxes (internal/controls/indicator `animateTo`): the dot pops from 0 on `spring.bounce` and out the same way; the check draws on `transition.check` and erases on `transition.uncheck` (with `checkAnimation="pop"`, pops from `scale.icon` out of a 2px blur on `spring.checkPop`, out on `transition.popOut`). shadcn/ui hides them as soon as they're unchecked: hold them `display: block` while they leave. The indicator's colors change on `transition.color`.
- Error: unfolds on `transition.disclosure` (350ms, `ease.soft`): height from 0 and margin-top from minus its parent's row gap to its own, out of `blur.disclosure` as it fades in; folds back held shown. Changed again on the way, from its current height, margin, opacity and blur. If its step's height is still gliding (a step glide, or its own earlier toggle's), stop that glide and take the step from its current height to its final one (with the error open or folded) on that same tween, with its own `min-height`/`max-height` animation (`fill: 'backwards'`, kept per step so the next toggle continues from it): the error is never clipped, then chased. In one batch of mutations, toggle the errors after the step change and the buttons: a leaving step's error is then measured while its step is shown, and an arriving step's error sees the step's glide.
- Previous, Skip, Next and Submit: in place, from `scale.action` (0.9, times their own scale) and `blur.icon` as they fade in, to their own resting opacity and scale (a disabled button's `opacity-50` stays 0.5), on `transition.swap` (150ms, `ease.out`); out the same way from it, held shown (still `inert`) and pinned where they were (boxes read in the old layout), so what stays doesn't move. Next and Submit share a grid cell: they swap through each other.
- Nothing on mount, nor before the first paint (`data-animate-ui-questionnaire-ready`): a resumed step and default answers show at rest. Reduced motion: the steps still go one after the other, opacity only on `transition.fade`, the height changing at once after the exit; the rest fades (the progress numbers crossfade). Every timing through `useTempoTransition`.

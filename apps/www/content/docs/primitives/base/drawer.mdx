## Usage

```tsx
<Drawer>
  <DrawerTrigger>Open Drawer</DrawerTrigger>
  <DrawerPortal>
    <DrawerBackdrop />
    <DrawerViewport>
      <DrawerPopup>
        <DrawerContent>
          <DrawerHeader>
            <DrawerTitle>Drawer Title</DrawerTitle>
            <DrawerDescription>Drawer Description</DrawerDescription>
          </DrawerHeader>
          <p>Drawer Content</p>
          <DrawerFooter>
            <DrawerClose>Close</DrawerClose>
          </DrawerFooter>
        </DrawerContent>
      </DrawerPopup>
    </DrawerViewport>
  </DrawerPortal>
</Drawer>
```

## Motion

The primitive animates with CSS transitions that read Base UI's own drawer state, so it needs no Motion and never fights the swipe gesture. Position the parts with your own classes; the motion classes are already on `DrawerBackdrop` and `DrawerPopup`.

- `DrawerPopup` rests at `translate(var(--drawer-swipe-movement-x), calc(var(--drawer-snap-point-offset) + var(--drawer-swipe-movement-y)))` and starts and ends 100% of its own size off the edge given by `data-swipe-direction`. It opens in 500ms and closes in `--drawer-swipe-strength × 300ms`, both with `ease-drawer`; transitions are off while `data-swiping`.
- `DrawerBackdrop` fades with `1 - --drawer-swipe-progress`: 300ms in, `--drawer-swipe-strength × 200ms` out, with `ease-out-strong`.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the popup fades in place; a swipe dismissal still follows the finger out.

The `ease-drawer` and `ease-out-strong` utilities come from `@animate-ui/lib-motion`, installed as a dependency.

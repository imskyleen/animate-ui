## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import {
  Tabs,
  TabsContent,
  TabsContents,
  TabsHighlight,
  TabsHighlightItem,
  TabsList,
  TabsTrigger,
} from '@animate-ui/vue/primitives/tabs';
</script>

<template>
  <Tabs default-value="account">
    <TabsHighlight class="absolute inset-0 rounded-md bg-background">
      <TabsList class="flex rounded-lg bg-muted p-1">
        <TabsHighlightItem value="account" class="flex-1">
          <TabsTrigger value="account">Account</TabsTrigger>
        </TabsHighlightItem>
        <TabsHighlightItem value="password" class="flex-1">
          <TabsTrigger value="password">Password</TabsTrigger>
        </TabsHighlightItem>
      </TabsList>
    </TabsHighlight>
    <TabsContents>
      <TabsContent value="account">Make changes to your account here.</TabsContent>
      <TabsContent value="password">Change your password here.</TabsContent>
    </TabsContents>
  </Tabs>
</template>
```

Unlike the library tabs, these are Animate UI's own: no reka-ui underneath. `TabsHighlight` and `TabsHighlightItem` are optional; without them, style the active trigger with `data-state="active"`. Without `default-value`, the first trigger is selected.

## Animation

- The highlight slides to the selected trigger on a spring (stiffness 300, damping 30): one surface is handed from the previous trigger to the new one, at full opacity ([Highlight](/docs/vue/primitives/animate-ui/highlight)).
- Panels sit side by side and slide to the selected one on a critically damped spring (stiffness 300, damping 35): they glide into place without bouncing past it. The container's height follows the active panel on the same spring. Mounted hidden (in a closed panel, or a tab waiting its turn), the container shows at its height once it's on screen, without growing in. Inactive panels blur (4px, spring with stiffness 300, damping 30) and are `inert`.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move to the next or previous enabled trigger (wrapping) and select it, Home and End jump to the ends; only the selected trigger is in the Tab order. Keyboard selection animates exactly like a click.
- `<AnimateUIProvider :tempo>` paces every spring.
- Under reduced motion, the highlight, panels and height change at once.

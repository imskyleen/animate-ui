## Animation

- Rockets launch from the bottom edge every 300 to 800ms (divided by `population`), trail upwards and burst into 50 to 150 particles that slow down, fall and fade.
- A click launches a rocket towards the pointer.
- The show takes place in a space as wide as the window, with the container's aspect ratio, scaled down to the container: in a container narrower than the window, rockets and particles are smaller and slower. The canvas is drawn at the device pixel ratio.
- The simulation advances one step per displayed frame, so it runs faster on 120Hz screens.
- It pauses off screen and while the tab is hidden: nothing launches and the last frame stays.
- Under reduced motion, it shows a still frame of three bursts and clicks do nothing.

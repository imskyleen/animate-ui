## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import StarsBackground from '@/components/animate-ui/backgrounds/stars/StarsBackground.vue';
</script>

<template>
  <section class="relative h-[480px] overflow-hidden">
    <StarsBackground class="absolute inset-0" />
  </section>
</template>
```

## Animation

- Three layers of stars (1, 2 and 3px) scroll upwards in a loop at different speeds, for depth: the smallest crosses 2000px in `speed` seconds, the others take two and three times longer. They move with hardware-accelerated `transform`s.
- The sky shifts away from the mouse on a spring (`factor`, `transition`), relative to the center of the container, for parallax. On touch screens, a tap shifts it too.
- The loops pause off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the stars hold still and there is no parallax.

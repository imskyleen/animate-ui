## Usage

```vue

import GravityStarsBackground from '@/components/animate-ui/backgrounds/gravity-stars/GravityStarsBackground.vue';
</script>

<template>
  <section class="relative h-[480px] overflow-hidden">
    <GravityStarsBackground class="absolute inset-0" />
  </section>
</template>
```

## Animation

- Stars drift across a canvas at `movementSpeed`, slow down little by little, wrap around its edges and glow.
- Near the pointer (`mouseInfluence`), they are pulled in or pushed away (`mouseGravity`) and glow brighter. The pointer's last position keeps acting after it leaves (before the first move, it is the top-left corner).
- The stars take the text color (`currentColor`), so they follow light and dark mode.
- The canvas is sized to its container at the device pixel ratio. A resize scatters the stars again.
- The simulation advances one step per displayed frame, so it runs faster on 120Hz screens.
- It pauses off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the stars hold still.

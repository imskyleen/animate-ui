## Usage

```vue

import GlyphBackground from '@/components/animate-ui/backgrounds/glyph/GlyphBackground.vue';
</script>

<template>
  <section class="relative h-[560px] overflow-hidden bg-[#121212]">
    <GlyphBackground class="absolute inset-0 text-white" />
  </section>
</template>
```

## Animation

- The background is a grid of random letters and digits in a monospace font, invisible until light falls on them. Now and then a character changes.
- At rest, soft glows made of characters drift slowly across it, about 30px a second towards the bottom left, reshaping as they go. Nothing else moves.
- Moving the cursor wakes the glows up all over the field, and a trail of light follows the cursor. Both settle back within a second of stillness.
- A click sends two waves out of the point, 0.3s apart, the second one dimmer. They spread fast then slower and slower (about 140px in 0.3s, 330px in a second), with a sharp outer edge fading inwards, their outline wavering into a heart or a blob, and fade out within 4s.
- The glows take `glowColor` and the waves `shapeColor`; both default to the container's text color.
- Drawn with WebGL, every character cell lit on the GPU, at most twice the device pixel ratio.
- The loop pauses off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the glows hold still and the cursor and clicks don't light anything.

## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import BubbleBackground from '@/components/animate-ui/backgrounds/bubble/BubbleBackground.vue';
</script>

<template>
  <section class="relative h-[480px] overflow-hidden">
    <BubbleBackground class="absolute inset-0" />
  </section>
</template>
```

## Animation

- Five blurred, color-blended bubbles move on slow loops: two drift 100px back and forth (30s and 40s, eased in and out), three orbit around points off to the side (full turns in 20s, 40s and 20s, linear).
- With `interactive`, a sixth bubble follows the mouse on a spring (`transition`), relative to the center of the container. On touch screens, a tap moves it too.
- The loops pause off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the bubbles hold a still frame and the follower stays put.

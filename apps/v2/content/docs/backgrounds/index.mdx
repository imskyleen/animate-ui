---
title: Backgrounds
description: Animated backgrounds for heroes and sections. Each one is a file in your project, added with the shadcn CLI.
---

Backgrounds aren't part of `@animate-ui/react`: like shadcn/ui components, each one is copied into your project, so it's yours to edit. Here, Bubble:

<RegistryInstallation name="backgrounds-bubble" />

They fill their parent: give it `position: relative` and a height, and the background `absolute inset-0`. They pause off screen and when the tab is hidden, and stay still under reduced motion.

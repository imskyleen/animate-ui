## Animation

- Vertical strings, `gap` pixels apart, sway in a light breeze: two slow waves run across them (`sway`). They fade out at the top and bottom edges.
- Sweeping the cursor across a string plucks it where you crossed it, as hard as you moved. Each string rings on its own damped Motion spring, low on the left and higher to the right like a harp (`tension`, `damping`), then settles.
- A string brightens, thickens and glows while it rings.
- A click (or a tap) plucks every string in turn, outwards from the point at 1100px/s, softer the farther away: a glissando.
- The strings take the container's text color, or `lineColor`. Drawn on a canvas, at most twice the device pixel ratio.
- The loop pauses off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the strings hang still and don't answer the cursor.

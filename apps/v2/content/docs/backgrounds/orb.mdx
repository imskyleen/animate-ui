## Usage

```tsx

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative h-[560px] overflow-hidden">
      <OrbBackground className="absolute inset-0" />
    </section>
  );
}
```

## Animation

- Inside the sphere, two blurred conic gradients of `colors` turn in opposite directions (24s and 38s a lap, divided by `speed`), so the colors keep mixing without ever repeating the same way.
- The halo around it breathes: it grows by 10% and brightens over 7s, back and forth.
- The light follows the cursor: the highlight slides towards it and the shade to the opposite side, and the orb leans up to 18px its way, on a spring (`transition`). Leaving the container brings them back to rest, lit from the upper left.
- The swirl and the halo are animated with hardware-accelerated `transform`s and `opacity`: each blurred layer is drawn once, then only turned.
- The loops pause off screen and while the tab is hidden.
- Under reduced motion, the orb holds still and its light doesn't follow the cursor.

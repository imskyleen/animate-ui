## Usage

```tsx
import {
  BottomNavigation,
  BottomNavigationAccessory,
  BottomNavigationHighlight,
  BottomNavigationHighlightItem,
  BottomNavigationItem,
  BottomNavigationLabel,
} from '@animate-ui/react/primitives/bottom-navigation';

const ITEMS = [
  { value: 'home', href: '/', label: 'Home', icon: <HomeIcon /> },
  { value: 'search', href: '/search', label: 'Search', icon: <SearchIcon /> },
];

export function AppNavigation() {
  return (
    <BottomNavigation
      defaultValue="home"
      scrollBehavior="minimize"
      aria-label="Main"
      className="fixed inset-x-0 bottom-4 mx-auto flex w-fit items-center gap-2.5"
    >
      <BottomNavigationHighlight className="absolute inset-x-0 inset-y-1 rounded-full bg-muted">
        <ul className="flex rounded-full bg-background px-1 ring-1 ring-border">
          {ITEMS.map((item) => (
            <li key={item.value}>
              <BottomNavigationHighlightItem value={item.value}>
                <BottomNavigationItem
                  value={item.value}
                  href={item.href}
                  className="flex h-[3.875rem] w-[4.875rem] flex-col items-center justify-center pt-[5px] data-[minimized]:size-12 data-[minimized]:pt-0"
                >
                  {item.icon}
                  <BottomNavigationLabel className="pt-[5px] text-xs">
                    {item.label}
                  </BottomNavigationLabel>
                </BottomNavigationItem>
              </BottomNavigationHighlightItem>
            </li>
          ))}
        </ul>
      </BottomNavigationHighlight>
      <BottomNavigationAccessory className="size-[3.875rem] rounded-full bg-background p-1 ring-1 ring-border data-[minimized]:size-12">
        <button className="size-full rounded-full hover:bg-muted">+</button>
      </BottomNavigationAccessory>
    </BottomNavigation>
  );
}
```

Items are links: give them an `href`, or `asChild` with your router's link or a `button`. The selected item gets `aria-current="page"` and `data-state="active"`. With a router, pass the current route as `value` so the highlight follows navigation. `BottomNavigationHighlight` and `BottomNavigationHighlightItem` are optional; without them, style the active item with `data-state="active"`.

`scrollBehavior` sets what the bar does while the page scrolls down: `"minimize"` shrinks it to its icons, like iOS' tab bar, `"hide"` slides it out, and `"none"` (the default) leaves it. It comes back as soon as the page scrolls up or reaches the top, an item takes keyboard focus, or the bar is tapped. It listens to the window, or to `scrollContainer` when the page scrolls inside an element.

While minimized, the items, the labels and the accessory get `data-minimized`: give the items and the accessory their minimized size with it (`data-[minimized]:size-12`). They ease between their two sizes, padding included, and the labels fold away.

`BottomNavigationLabel` and `BottomNavigationAccessory` are optional:

- A label shows on every item. With `show="active"`, it shows only on the selected item, beside its icon, so the bar stays icons and one name. Its text stays in the accessibility tree, so it names the item even while folded.
- The accessory (a button, a player, a search field) stays beside the bar and minimizes with it. Pressing or focusing it leaves the bar minimized.

Under its icon (an item laid out in a column), a label folds its height; beside it, its width. A folded label takes no room: put its spacing inside it (`pt-[5px]`, `pl-2`), not on the item.

## Animation

- The highlight slides to the selected item on a spring (stiffness 300, damping 30): one surface is handed from the previous item to the new one, at full opacity ([Highlight](/docs/primitives/animate-ui/highlight)).
- Items scale to 0.97 while pressed, on the buttons' spring.
- With `show="active"`, labels open on the highlight's spring, so the highlight widens with the selected item: the new label grows from nothing out of a 4px blur as the previous one narrows and blurs away.
- Both scroll behaviors start once the page has scrolled down by 6px and run on a critically damped spring (stiffness 300, damping 35).
  - `"minimize"`: the items and the accessory ease to their minimized size and padding, the highlight staying on the selected item. Labels fade out into a 4px blur in 0.1s and fold away on the same spring, and fade back in over 0.2s; beside their icons, they fade with their width. Tapped while minimized, an item grows the bar and takes the selection in one move.
  - `"hide"`: the bar slides out past the bottom edge, and back on the way up.
- `<AnimateUIProvider tempo>` paces every spring.
- Under reduced motion, the highlight moves at once, items don't scale, labels open and fold at once, the bar minimizes at once, and the hidden bar fades out instead of sliding (and can’t be clicked while hidden).

## Usage

```tsx
<Slider defaultValue={[25, 75]} max={100}>
  <SliderTrack>
    <SliderRange />
  </SliderTrack>
  <SliderThumb aria-label="Minimum" />
  <SliderThumb aria-label="Maximum" />
</Slider>
```

## Motion

Dragging is direct manipulation: the thumb and range follow the pointer with no transition, and arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End change the value instantly. Pressing the track away from the thumb (a jump) springs the thumb and range to the new value with `spring.snappy`. Radix positions them with `left`/`right` percentages, so the layout jumps and a FLIP transform (a translate for the thumb, a translate and scale from the start edge for the range) springs back to identity; it works for range sliders and vertical sliders. If the pointer moves more than 3px after the press, the animation is cancelled at once and the thumb sits under the pointer. While a thumb is pressed it scales to 1.15. Under reduced motion nothing moves or scales: every change is instant.

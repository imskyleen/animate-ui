## Usage

```tsx
<label htmlFor="otp">Verification code</label>
<OTPField id="otp" length={6}>
  <OTPFieldInput>
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
  <OTPFieldInput aria-label="Character 2 of 6">
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
  <OTPFieldInput aria-label="Character 3 of 6">
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
  <OTPFieldSeparator />
  <OTPFieldInput aria-label="Character 4 of 6">
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
  <OTPFieldInput aria-label="Character 5 of 6">
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
  <OTPFieldInput aria-label="Character 6 of 6">
    <OTPFieldCaret />
  </OTPFieldInput>
</OTPField>
```

## Motion

Each `OTPFieldInput` keeps Base UI's real `<input>`, hides its glyph (`-webkit-text-fill-color: transparent`) and renders an animated copy of the character on top of it (`data-slot="otp-field-char"`, `aria-hidden`). A typed character fades in from a 0.95 scale with `transition.press` (0.15s): keystroke feedback stays fast. A pasted or autofilled code staggers its characters by 25ms. Deleting is instant, and characters that only shift because one before them was removed don't animate. Focus moves between inputs instantly, since it follows the keyboard. `OTPFieldCaret` blinks with a CSS animation while its input is focused and empty, and stops blinking under reduced motion; characters then only fade, without scaling or staggering.

## Accessibility

Each slot is Base UI's own `<input>`: labelling, keyboard navigation, paste, autofill (`autocomplete="one-time-code"`) and form submission are unchanged. The animated characters are `aria-hidden`; the value always lives in the inputs. With `mask`, the animated copy shows a bullet.
